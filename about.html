Legacy of Trust

15+ Years of Transforming Bengaluru's Skyline

Founded in 2011 by visionary engineers in Nagarbhavi, Satva Developers has grown into one of Karnataka's most trusted real estate brands. We specialize in luxury residential towers, independent villa townships, and Grade-A commercial spaces.

Mission

To deliver sustainable, RERA-approved luxury homes with zero compromise on engineering standard and customer delight.

Vision

To lead the South Indian real estate sector through smart architectural design, green technology, and transparent corporate governance.
